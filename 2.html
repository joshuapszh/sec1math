➗ 4. The In-Betweeners

Purpose: A quick-reference guide for handling fractions and decimals. These rules are crucial for maintaining accuracy during complex multi-step problems.

🍕

Fractions

  • • Add/Sub: You MUST find a common denominator (LCM) first.
  • • Mult/Div: Convert mixed numbers to improper. To divide, use Keep-Change-Flip.
🎯

Decimals

  • • Add/Sub: ALWAYS align the decimal points vertically. Pad with zeroes.
  • • Multiply: Multiply normally first, then count total decimal places to put back.
  • • Divide: Shift the decimal point in BOTH until the divisor is a whole number.
