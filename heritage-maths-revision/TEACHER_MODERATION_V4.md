- The draft question/answer change invalidates earlier version-mismatched saved scores through the existing progress signature mechanism.

## Release concerns
- Generic provisional M/A criteria must be rewritten to align precisely with each problem's valid methods, follow-through rules and units.
- The automatic score still awards entire part marks for the final answer alone.
- The 50:40:10 difficulty weighting is numerically correct, but challenge depth and mark realism are **not** pedagogically validated.
- A 1 d.p. display of 45.0% is required in the written key; the numeric checker accepts 45 and 45.0 as equivalent, so formatting is not auto-marked.
- Original confidential SA2 diagram/equation comparison and teacher approval remain outstanding.

## Suggested review process
For every item, check the stimulus, arithmetic, expected written method, alternative routes, units, answer precision, dependency across parts, and perceived challenge. Approve only after a complete browser regression on this revision.
