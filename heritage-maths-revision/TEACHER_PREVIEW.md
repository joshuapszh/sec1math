# Teacher preview instructions

**Draft only. Do not assign to students or link from the live homepage.**

## Preview locally
1. In GitHub open the `development/heritage-maths-revision` branch.
2. Select **Code → Download ZIP**, extract the repository, then open a terminal in its top-level folder.
3. Start `python -m http.server 8000`.
4. In your laptop browser visit `http://localhost:8000/heritage-maths-revision/`.
5. Try each of the 12 level/paper/set choices, the hints and solutions, graph illustrations, and CSV report.

## Current scope
- 12 selectable practice sets (10 questions and 50 marks per set).
- Secondary 1 Paper 1 Set 1 is an individually authored reference draft.
- The other 11 sets are parametric **drafts** with repeated structural patterns.
- Numerical and restricted algebraic answer checking; graphical illustrations and an unscored graph laboratory.
- Progress is saved in the browser on the same laptop only.

## Important limitations
- The generated questions and explanations are not independently teacher-approved.
- Partial method credit is not assessed automatically.
- Some answers requiring significant figures are only value-checked, not presentation-checked.
- Automated tests are regression checks, not evidence of exhaustive mathematical correctness or full examination-confidentiality clearance.
- Do not upload or commit confidential SA2 examination papers, solutions, or detailed overlap matrices.

## Simple teacher acceptance check
For each set inspect every question, recompute the answers independently, test one incorrect answer and a hint, verify each diagram and unit, then compare privately with school exam material. Record corrections before approving student use.
