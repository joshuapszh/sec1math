# Teacher rubric status
Question-specific method marks require teacher review. Automatic final-answer scoring is separate.

## Draft question-specific marking points

| Question | Part (a) | Part (b) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Divide by 12 (M1); 36 (A1) | Scale to seven cartons (M1); multiply 36 by 7 (M1); 252 (A1) |
| Q2 | Perimeter relation (M1); 18 cm (A1) | Form x + 4 = 18 (M1); isolate x (M1); 14 (A1) |
| Q3 | Eight parts equal 72 (M1); 9 (A1) | Identify three parts (M1); multiply by nine (M1); 27 (A1) |
| Q4 | Calculate 15% of 120 (M1); $18 (A1) | Subtract discount (M1); correct calculation (M1); $102 (A1) |
| Q5 | Add volumes (M1); 84 litres (A1) | Combine durations (M1); total volume divided by time (M1); 16.8 litres/min (A1) |
| Q6 | Identify difference 5 (M1); form seventh plus five times (n−1) (M1); 5n+2 (A1) | Substitute term position 15 (M1); 77 (A1) |
| Q7 | Calculate outer rectangle (M1); 240 m² (A1) | Square area 36 (M1); subtract it (M1); 204 m² (A1) |
| Q8 | Order scores (M1); median 11 (A1) | Sum scores to 78 (M1); divide by seven (M1); 11.1 to three significant figures (A1) |
| Q9 | Half base times height (M1); 48 cm² (A1) | Cross-section times length (M1); substitute 48 and 15 (M1); 720 cm³ (A1) |
| Q10 | Add both sales groups (M1); revenue $290 (A1) | Find cost and profit (M1); profit as share of cost (M1); 45.0% (A1) |

M = method, A = accuracy. These are proposed teacher-facing marking points only. Equivalent valid methods should be accepted. Follow-through method credit may apply when a later calculation consistently uses an incorrect earlier result; teacher judgment is necessary. Units and specified rounding must be checked manually. This table is not fed into automated grading.
