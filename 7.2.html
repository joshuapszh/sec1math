Chapter 7: Number Patterns

Section 7.2: General Term in a Number Pattern/Sequence

🎯 Chapter Targets:
  • Find a formula (an algebraic expression) for the general term (nth term) of a sequence.
📚 Concept Reference: General Terms

• Finding the 50th or 100th term by continuing a sequence manually is too tedious and time-consuming.
• The general term or nth term is an algebraic expression that allows us to find the value of any term in a sequence instantly.

The Formula:
• Use Tn = a + (n-1)d
• a = 1st term
• d = common difference (T2 - T1)

CRITICAL NOTE: We can only use this formula if the common difference (d) is a constant (i.e., any term minus its previous term gives the same number).
