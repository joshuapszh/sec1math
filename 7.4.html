Chapter 7: Number Patterns

Section 7.4: Using Number Patterns in Real-World Contexts

🎯 Chapter Targets:
  • Use number patterns in real-world contexts.
  • Translate visual and situational models into algebraic general terms.
📚 Concept Reference: Real-World Contexts

• Mathematics models the real world. Visual patterns (like chemical bonds, architectural shapes, or seating arrangements) can be translated directly into number sequences.
• Always build a table of values first to find your First term (a) and your Common difference (d).
• Apply the formula Tn = a + (n-1)d to create a rule that governs the physical system.
