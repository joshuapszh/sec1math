Chapter 7: Number Patterns

Section 7.3: Problem Solving Strategies

🎯 Chapter Targets:
  • Use number patterns to solve complex arithmetic sums efficiently.
  • Apply structured problem-solving heuristics.
📚 Concept Reference: The Four-Step Process

When encountering a complex mathematical problem, apply this four-step heuristic:
1. Understand the problem: What are the unknowns? What information is given?
2. Devise a plan: Choose a strategy. For number patterns, the best strategies are usually to look for a pattern, simplify the problem, or write an equation.
3. Carry out the plan: Execute your strategy step-by-step.
4. Looking back: Check that your solution makes sense and see if it can be extended to a more general formula.

Crucial Formula Derived from Looking Back:
Sum of the first n integers (1 + 2 + 3 + ... + n) = n(n+1) / 2
