📚 5. Quick Reference Guides

➗ Algebraic Fractions

Treat them exactly like primary school fractions.

  • Rule 1: To Add/Subtract, you MUST find a Common Denominator (LCM) first.
  • Rule 2: Expand the top numerators carefully once combined.
  • (2x)/3 + (x)/5 → (10x)/15 + (3x)/15 → 13x/15

⬅️ 6. Factorisation

The "Reverse Expansion". Putting brackets back in by finding the Highest Common Factor (HCF).

  • Rule 1: Find the largest number AND letter that divides into all terms.
  • Rule 2: Pull the HCF outside, write what's left inside the brackets.
  • 4ax - 16ay → HCF is 4a → 4a(x - 4y)
